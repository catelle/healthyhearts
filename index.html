ABOUT THE VIEW

Healthy Hearts Cameroon

This dashboard follows the map-first interaction pattern of the reference platform and translates the supplied technical brief into a decision-support view.

National indicators are taken directly from the PDF specification. The Cameroon region boundaries come from geoBoundaries ADM1. The PDF does not contain area-level totals, so no health-area result is fabricated.
